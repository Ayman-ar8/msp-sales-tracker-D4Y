# Start here

1. On GitHub, click **Use this template** to make your own copy of this
   repo.
2. Make sure Claude has a **Google Workspace MCP** connected with Drive,
   Sheets and Forms. Setup checks this first and won't start without it.
3. Open your copy in Claude Code and say:

   > Set up my sales tracker

Your assistant introduces itself, asks you to name it, and walks you
through everything. You'll need to click things in three places: Google,
Meta (optional) and Vercel. It tells you exactly what to click and checks
each step before moving on.

What you'll end up with:
- End-of-day forms for closers and setters.
- A master Google Sheet that you own, with automatic backups.
- Meta ad spend pulled in every hour.
- A password-protected dashboard on Vercel.
- `MY-SOP.md`: your own guide with your links.
